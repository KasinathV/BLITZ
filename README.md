SafePath AI — Our AI-powered MCP server helps women find safer routes by combining AI risk analysis, street lighting, safe places, and community reports.

Hey r/mcptothemoon 👋 — We built SafePath AI, an MCP (Model Context Protocol) server that helps people—especially women—choose safer routes instead of simply the fastest ones. Our AI evaluates multiple route options using safety signals such as lighting conditions, nearby police stations, hospitals, 24×7 safe places, and community-reported incidents, then explains why a route is safer.

💡 What it does

SafePath AI analyzes multiple route options and generates a safety score for each one. It:

* 🛣️ Recommends the safest route based on multiple safety factors.
* 💡 Identifies well-lit and poorly lit road segments.
* 🛡️ Shows nearby police stations and emergency services.
* 📍 Highlights safe places such as hospitals, pharmacies, and 24×7 stores.
* 🔮 Predicts potentially risky areas using AI.
* ✍️ Accepts anonymous community incident reports to improve future route recommendations.
* 🆘 Supports an emergency SOS workflow for sharing critical information.

Rather than simply returning a route, our AI explains why it chose that route, helping users make informed decisions.

🛠️ How it works

SafePath AI runs as an MCP server, allowing any MCP-compatible client (Claude, Cursor, ChatGPT-compatible MCP clients, and others) to access its tools.

Our MCP tools include:

* Safe Route Generator
* Street Lighting Analysis
* Nearby Police Finder
* Safe Place Finder
* AI Risk Prediction
* Safety Score Generator
* Incident Reporting
* SOS Assistant

Built and deployed on Nitrostack, making it easy to host, share, and integrate with existing AI workflows.
